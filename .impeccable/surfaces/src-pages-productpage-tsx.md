# ProductPage Surface Brief

## Scope & Visitor Mode
Surface: `src/pages/ProductPage.tsx`
Mode: Persuade & Operate
Audience: Hospitality procurement directors, F&B managers, chefs, and housekeeping heads sourcing commercial supplies.
Job: Browse department lines, compare commercial specifications, and assemble bulk multi-item quotes quickly.
Primary Action: Explore verified product lines, inspect on-card specs, and add items to the interactive RFQ Tray.

## Strategy & Key Moments
- Memorable moment: Tactile, expansive High-Impact Showcase cards that surface alloy/material grades, carton packing counts, and certifications immediately with zero squinting, coupled with an architectural department header and high-contrast Turnkey Bundle card.
- Replaced anti-reference: Cramped cards with 11-13px washed-out grey text, hidden specifications, and low-contrast grey badges.

## Direction contract

### THESIS
The hospitality catalog owns immediate, high-contrast legibility and physical authority, refusing the industry rut of cramped, washed-out e-commerce cards with hidden specs and microscopic typography.

### OWN-WORLD
Deep architectural navy (`#0c1624`, `#02348d`) with crisp porcelain white surfaces (`#ffffff`), solid dark ink typography (`#0f1c2d`, `#1e293b`), vibrant crimson accent badges (`#d91b2a`), structured dual-border card framing, and tactile rounded pill filters.

### STORY
The buyer arrives at an operational department, immediately grasps commercial pedigree and verified brand authority, reads real material specs and pack counts directly on large cards, and effortlessly bundles quotes into an RFQ tray.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
A confident architectural masthead (42px display heading, department metrics), large tactile department switcher tabs (16px bold with high-contrast active states), an executive Turnkey Procurement Suite card with crisp green checkmarks and rapid dispatch guarantee, followed by comfortable 15px category filter pills and a 340px spacious search bar.
