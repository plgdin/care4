# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users
Hotels, fine dining restaurants, banquet halls, cafes, catering companies, and institutional facilities sourcing commercial-grade hospitality supplies. Primary decision makers include F&B directors, executive chefs, procurement heads, general managers, and housekeeping directors.

## Product Purpose
Care4 Associates provides comprehensive, end-to-end B2B procurement for hospitality operations—supplying everything from European vitrified tableware and precision cutlery to commercial back-of-house kitchen equipment, luxury guest linens, and institutional sanitation chemicals.

## Positioning
Single-source hospitality supplier combining verified Tier-1 brands (Dinewell, Borosil, Ariane, Diversey, Cambro, FNS, Ocean) with turnkey opening suites, transparent commercial specifications, and dedicated wholesale logistics.

## Operating Context
Procurement teams evaluate catalog items across operational departments (Fine Dining & Banquets, Cafe & Beverage, Commercial Kitchen, Housekeeping & Suites). They require immediate verification of material grades (e.g., AISI 304 18/10 stainless, super-vitrified bone china, 100% melamine, NSF certified), carton packing counts, and rapid multi-item RFQ (Request for Quote) aggregation.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Curated multi-department catalog (68+ product lines across 9 distinct categories).
- Interactive RFQ Inquiry Tray allowing multi-item quote aggregation and single-click quote dispatch.
- Detailed technical spec modal and par-level guidelines for hospitality operators.
- Web-based responsive application built with React, Vite, and modern CSS.

## Brand Commitments
- Brand: Care4 Associates ("Hospitality Refined").
- Palette: Deep Trust Navy (`#02348d`, `#0c1624`), Crimson Accent (`#d91b2a`), Crisp High-Contrast Neutral Whites/Off-whites (`#ffffff`, `#f8fafc`, text `#0f1c2d`).
- Typography: Modern geometric and humanist sans-serifs (`Lexend Deca`, `Geist`, `Inter`) prioritizing high legibility, strong scale hierarchy, and institutional gravitas.

## Evidence on Hand
- Full product catalog with real commercial specs (`src/data/siteData.ts`).
- High-resolution photography of tableware, kitchen utensils, banquets, and housekeeping suites (`public/images/`).
- Verified brand affiliations and client roster.

## Product Principles
1. Immediate Legibility: Technical specifications, materials, and packing details must be clear, crisp, and high-contrast at comfortable font sizes without squinting.
2. High-Impact Visual Authority: Generous product imagery and structured hierarchy communicate commercial grade durability and luxury hospitality standards.
3. Frictionless Procurement: Effortless department switching, clear category filtering, rapid instant search, and one-tap RFQ tray assembly.
